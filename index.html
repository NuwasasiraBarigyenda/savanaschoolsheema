Frequently asked questions

Which classes does the school have?

The school supports both O & A levels.

Do you offer boarding?

Yes, day and boarding options are available for both levels but all candidates are required to be in boarding section. Ask the administrator about requirements.

You can pay o the school payment mobile number 0782492279 in the names of Tumusiime Barigyenda or Message the school administrator on WhatsApp or call the office. We will share the current amounts and payment details.

Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
